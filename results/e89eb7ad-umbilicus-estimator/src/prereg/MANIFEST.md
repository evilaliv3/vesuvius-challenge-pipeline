# Pre-registrations of the umbilicus score patch: what each is, when it was written, and its hash

The paper cites two pre-registrations and both are in this folder. Each states in its own first
lines the day and the hour it was written, and those lines are the record: they were written
before the measurement they govern, and nothing in them was adjusted afterwards.

The sizes and digests below are of the files as they stand here, so that a reader can check the
copy they are holding against the one the article was built from.

## What each says, and when it was written

**`preregistration-fifteen-references.md`**, written 2026-09-15 15:33:39Z. The pre-registration of
the fifteen manual references: the two blocks, the metric, the criteria P1, P2, C1 and C2 and the
0.30 mm threshold, all fixed before the grids of the eleven new scrolls were downloaded.

**`note-density-bias.md`**, written 2026-09-16 10:54Z. The note on the density bias of the weighted
sum, post hoc with respect to the frozen paper and saying in its own first paragraph what was
already known when it was written.

## Documents cited inside these two

Both cite work that is not carried here: earlier reports and pre-registrations of the same line of
work, and the tables of a synthetic battery run before either was written. Where the article does
not carry the document, the text says what it is and when it was written rather than pointing at a
file a reader cannot open. Everything either pre-registration relies on that a reader has to be
able to check is in this folder, under `evidence/` and `inputs/`.
