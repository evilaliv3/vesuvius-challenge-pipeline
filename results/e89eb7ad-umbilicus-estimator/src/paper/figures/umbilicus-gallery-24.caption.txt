One section of every scroll of the generalisation run, 24 panels sorted by scroll id. Grey: the segments of that scroll's normal grid on the slice named in the title, with the bounds of the grid as a dark grey square; each panel is drawn in the units of its own grid, whose widths run from 3,941 to 12,145 pixels, with a margin of 8 % of the width on every side. Dark grey circle: the estimate of the published estimator, the weighted mean. Blue circle: the estimate with the division removed, the weighted sum. Yellow cross: the reference umbilicus at that slice, drawn only on the 14 scrolls that have one, published by the challenge on PHerc. 0125, PHerc. 0211, PHerc. 0332, PHerc. 0826 and annotated by a third party on PHerc. 0191, PHerc. 0257, PHerc. 0268, PHerc. 0358, PHerc. 0800, PHerc. 0813, PHerc. 1203, PHerc. 1218, PHerc. 1447, PHerc. 1545; the reference was used only to measure and never to choose anything, and the 10 scrolls PHerc. 0175A, PHerc. 0175B, PHerc. 0306B, PHerc. 0343, PHerc. 0483A, PHerc. 0483B, PHerc. 0490A, PHerc. 0490B, PHerc. 0846A, PHerc. 0846B have none. Dark grey arrow: the published estimate lies beyond the window, in the direction the arrow leaves the blue circle, at the labelled distance between the two estimates, in millimetres where the voxel size of the scan is on record and in grid widths otherwise; this happens on 11 of the 24 panels. On the slices drawn, the published estimate lies outside its grid on 11 panels, PHerc. 0125, PHerc. 0211, PHerc. 0306B, PHerc. 0358, PHerc. 0483A, PHerc. 0490B, PHerc. 0846A, PHerc. 0846B, PHerc. 1203, PHerc. 1218, PHerc. 1545, and the estimate with the division removed lies inside on every panel. No walk in this run was stopped by a cap: the estimates are those of the compiled function, which has no iteration limit, and every walk stopped by itself. Slice rule: of the 24 slices the run sampled on each scroll, evenly spaced along z, the panel shows the twelfth in order of z, the lower of the two that straddle the middle of the sampled band; no slice was chosen by looking at it. Over the whole run, 576 of 576 estimates fall inside the volume with the division removed, against 350 of 576 as published. Drawn by tools/figure_umbilicus_gallery.py from evidence/cpp-positions-24.csv, the cached grids of the run and the reference files; no estimate was recomputed.
