# Data licence and citation

The code in this repository is MIT (see `LICENSE`). The **data is not ours** and keeps its own
licence.

`examples/`, `reference/`, and everything derived from them in `results/` come from the Vesuvius
Challenge open-data bucket, which is published under **CC BY-NC 4.0**: attribution required,
non-commercial use only. That covers the four published umbilici, the slices of the published
surface predictions, and the control points and error tables computed from them.

Cite the data as the organizers ask:

> Giorgio Angelotti, Stephen Parsons, Sean Johnson, Elian Rafael Dal Pra, Johannes Rudolph,
Paul Tafforeau, Alessandro Mirone, et al. *Vesuvius Challenge - CT Scans of Herculaneum
Papyri*. Vesuvius Challenge. 2026

Registry entry: <https://registry.opendata.aws/vesuvius-challenge-herculaneum-scrolls/>
Citation instructions: <https://github.com/ScrollPrize/open-data#citation>

If you use this bench in something commercial, the MIT code is yours to use, but the sample data
and the derived tables are not: regenerate them from a source you are allowed to use, or ask the
Vesuvius Challenge.
