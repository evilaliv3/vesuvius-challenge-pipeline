## The cut

On the square, z decreases strictly with the row i (checked on the geometry before this file: every dz/di < 0). An axial cut at
integer z = zc is therefore one node per column j: i*(j) is where the column's z crosses zc (linear interpolation between the
two rows that bracket it), the node is P bilinear at (i*, j) with its z set to zc. Columns whose z range does not contain zc give
no node. Nodes are ordered by j, spaced about 4 voxels. The in plane normal n at a node is perpendicular to the xy tangent
(central difference over j +- 2). All of this is inside the square only.

Profile at a node: raw value at offsets o = -40 .. +40 voxels (step 1) along n, in the planes zc - 1, zc, zc + 1, averaged over
the three planes, then smoothed with a Gaussian of sigma 1 voxel along o and a running mean over 3 nodes along the cut.
A node is not measurable if any value in o = -8 .. +8 is not measurable; not measurable nodes are counted, never scored as 0.

## The rule for «one layer», per cut

Per measurable node:
- d = the offset in -6 .. +6 of the maximum of the smoothed profile (the brightest band along the local normal).
- on_layer = |d| <= 3.
- centre_dark = smoothed value at o = 0 below T = 52.5.

The measure: share_on_layer = on_layer nodes / measurable nodes.

Crossing detection, both on the ordered nodes:
- (F) band flip: dm = running median of d over 5 nodes (about 20 voxels). A flip is a place where dm goes from >= +3 to <= -3,
  or from <= -3 to >= +3, within 3 consecutive nodes: the brightest band has passed from one side of the surface to the other,
  that is the surface has crossed the midline between two bands. Flips closer than 10 nodes are one event.
- (G) dark gap: a run of consecutive centre_dark nodes of 3 or more (N = 8 voxels: 3 nodes span more than 8 voxels of cut).
  A gap is resolved as a change of band if the median d of the 5 nodes before and the 5 after differ by 6 voxels or more, or
  their dm have opposite signs with both |dm| >= 3. Otherwise the gap is unresolved: the profile cannot tell whether the band on
  the far side is the same sheet.

Verdict per cut:
- no: at least one flip (F) or one gap resolved as a change of band (G), or share_on_layer < 0.50.
- yes: share_on_layer >= 0.80, no flip, no gap of 3 or more nodes, and the detector passed its positive reference on this cut
  (below).
- undecided otherwise, with the reason (share between 0.50 and 0.80, or an unresolved gap, or the reference failed, or fewer
  than 50 measurable nodes).

Overall: yes if every cut is yes; no if any cut is no; undecided otherwise. My look at every PNG is written in a separate
column (eye) and does not change a rule verdict; where the eye disagrees with the rule, that is written, not reconciled.

## References, paired with every cut (the same code, the same cut)

- Null (negative): the same nodes shifted by +6 voxels along n (about half the layer pitch, 103 to 140 um, 11 to 15 voxels).
  Its share_on_layer is written beside the cut's. If the null's share is 0.60 or more, the measure does not discriminate on
  that cut and the cut is undecided.
- Positive reference for the flip detector: the cut's own nodes with a synthetic ramp of +12 voxels along n over 10 nodes
  (about 40 voxels of cut) centred on the middle node, flat after it. The ramp crosses a band by construction when the pitch is
  11 to 15 voxels; the detector must report at least one flip within 10 nodes of the ramp. If it does not, «no flip» on that
  cut is not a pass (undecided).
- Our 800 sheets in the plane (nodes within 1 voxel of zc within 40 voxels of the cut) are drawn and their offsets along n
  written as a count; they are not a gate, since they can be wrong too.

## Addition of 2026-09-30T09:06:20Z: rule A does not discriminate; rule B (band drift) declared before its numbers

Why (from evidence/cuts.csv, written 09:02Z to 09:04Z): rule A's share_on_layer is 0.4102 to 0.5455 on the nine cuts and its
null (the same nodes shifted 6 voxels) is 0.4080 to 0.5434; on the four crack cuts the null is higher than the surface. The

verdicts stay as written («no» on all nine by its letter) and are reported with this note: the letter «no» rests on a measure
that its own references show does not separate the surface from a surface half a pitch off. Rule A is not rewritten.

Rule B, on the same profiles (scratch/profiles-*.npz, raw, o = -40 .. +40, nodes 4 voxels apart), tool tools/drift.py:
- Structure tensor of the strip (gradients per voxel, s spacing 4; Gaussian smoothing sigma 2 nodes along s and 2 voxels along
  o). Band slope m = -Jso / Joo (voxels of o per voxel of s); at a node, m_s = median of m over o = -4 .. +4; coherence
  c = sqrt((Jss - Joo)^2 + 4 Jso^2) / (Jss + Joo) at o = 0.
- Void node: the maximum over o = -3 .. +3 of the profile smoothed as in rule A is below T = 52.5 (the surface is not within 3
  voxels of anything as bright as the dark threshold).
- A node is measurable for B if its profile is measurable in -8 .. +8, it is not void, and c >= 0.2.
- Band drift D: cumulative sum of 4 x m_s over consecutive measurable nodes; a non measurable node ends a segment and D
  restarts. D is how far, in voxels along the normal, the band the surface sat on at the segment's start has moved away from
  the surface.
- Pitch p per cut: median over measurable nodes of the first autocorrelation peak of the detrended profile at lags 6 .. 25
  voxels; not measurable if fewer than 50 nodes give a peak.
- Per segment of 25 nodes or more (100 voxels): excursion E = max D - min D.
- Verdict per cut: no if any segment has E >= p (the surface has moved at least one full pitch across the bands) or a run of
  25 or more void nodes (100 voxels or more of the cut off papyrus); yes if every segment of 25 or more nodes has E < p / 2, no
  void run of 25, measurable nodes >= 80 per cent, and both references pass; undecided otherwise, with the reason.
- References: (1) the ramp of rule A (12 voxels over 10 nodes at the middle): D over nodes m - 10 .. m + 10 of the ramped
  profiles minus the unramped must change by 12 +- 4 voxels, else B does not see a known crossing on that cut (undecided).
  (2) Our 800 sheets: per sheet, its marks with |o| <= 15 in runs of 25 or more nodes (holes of up to 3 nodes bridged); for a
  run, r = (o_sheet(last) - o_sheet(first)) - (D(last) - D(first)) over the run's measurable nodes in one segment. Median |r|
  over runs is written; if it exceeds p / 2 the cut is undecided (D disagrees with sheets traced along the bands).
- Overall as before. Two more boxes per cut: centred on the middle of the longest void run and on the node of largest |D - D at
  segment start|, when they exist. evidence/drift.csv holds rule B's rows; evidence/verdict.csv is rewritten by drift.py with
  both rules' verdicts side by side and the eye column; the final column is rule B's verdict.

- A run: one of our 800 sheets' marks (cuts.py to_strip) with |o| <= 15, per node the median o, holes of up to 3 nodes bridged,
