**Definition.** On a sheet's own lattice (certified_region.lattice_from_patch, square.py's i = rint(x - min x),
j = rint(y - min y)), a cell is **certified** when it is covered (a lattice point of the sheet: no hole) and it is none of
(i) a v2 cell: its nearest working cell (rint(i / s), rint(j / s), area90.nearest_block, s the working stride, 2 on these
sheets) is marked crossed or jumped by sheet_cross_v2.pair_v2 and marks against any partner (area90.thresholds);
(ii) a self conflict cell: its nearest working cell is in lamina.conflicts(W, W, p, same=True) at the pitch of
area-0826-90/evidence/pitch.csv (15.125 voxels); (iii) an a2 flagged cell: area90.prep's a2cell (the 8 x 8 block of each
stride 8 evaluation point whose angle to the prediction's normal is above T*, or a cluster rule hole, taxicab 2 around
clusters above S). The **certified square** is square.py largest_square on the certified cells, in mm by the smaller of
step_i_mm and step_j_mm of the sheet's squares row. Written beside it: the hole-free square (largest_square on covered
cells; must equal the squares row, a known reference), and the squares avoiding v2 only, self conflict only, a2 only.

nearest working cell it is (about s x s = 4 cells each). The one exception so far is check95_box.py's and checks_file.py's
column square_with_itself_working_cells (799 for seed2604 C80), which counts WORKING cells (stride 2) and says so in its
header; certified-squares.csv carries full lattice cells only, and a column working_stride.
