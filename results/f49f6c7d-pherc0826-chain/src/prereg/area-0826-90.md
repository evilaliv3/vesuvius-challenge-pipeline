## Definitions

- **Cell area** a_c of a lattice cell, mm2: `coverage-union-1447/tools/union_area.py` `local_area`, imported, with its
  module voxel set to the PHerc0826 voxel above (|d_i x d_j| x voxel^2, central differences, one sided at an edge, the
  sheet's median steps product where a cell has no neighbour along an axis). Steps measured per sheet by square.py
  `median_step`, never typed.
- **a2 flagged cells** of a sheet: the stride 8 evaluation points of `a2_measure` (stevens-remedy-half-on/tools/a2_after.py,
  the same sequence of calls, reproduced so that the flag grid is kept) whose angle between the sheet normal and the
  prediction's normal is above T*; a lattice cell (i, j) is a2 flagged when its nearest stride point (8 rint(i / 8),
  8 rint(j / 8)) is flagged (the 8 x 8 block centred on the point), and also when it is a cluster rule hole
  (`cluster_rule.rule_holes`, taxicab 2 around points of clusters above S). Points not measurable are not flagged.
  **Known reference**: for every sheet, a2_measurable and a2_flagged equal, as strings, the row of
  `chain-0826/evidence/a2-cluster/<seed>.csv`; any difference refuses the study.
- **v2 crossing cells**: for each sheet A and every other sheet B whose 64 voxel bin keys meet A's dilated keys (the test
  of `sheet_cross.candidates`, done through one key to sheets index built from the same `keys_of`; equal by construction,
  checked against the brute test on 10 sheets, a column), `pair_v2(A, B)` and `marks` with L2_cross and L2_jump read from
  `sheet-crossing-map-1447/evidence/v2-thresholds.csv`. Those thresholds are lengths in PHerc1447 voxels (8.64 um, from
  voxel.py); here they are converted through millimetres to PHerc0826 voxels (times 8.64 / 9.362, both read), which makes
  the threshold shorter in voxels and marks at least as many lines as the unconverted value would. The other constants of
  the tool (overlap 10, floor 5, coincide 3 voxels, blob 4096 square voxels, radius 24) stay in voxels as written. A lattice
  cell is a v2 crossing cell when its working cell (rint(i / s), rint(j / s), s the working stride) is marked crossed or
  jumped against any partner.
- **Clean cells** of a sheet: covered cells that are neither a2 flagged nor v2 crossing cells.
- **Dedup, three rules, all on clean cells only** (an unclean cell of one sheet never hides a clean cell of another). Sheets
  are ordered by the seed order of per-seed-queue.csv, then k. A clean cell of sheet s is a duplicate when a clean cell of
  an earlier sheet (any seed, the same seed included) lies within r of it in 3D:
  - `half-step`: r = 0.5 x the smaller median step of sheet s (the rule of the order);
  - `one-step`: r = 1.0 x that step (why: two copies of the same papyrus sampled on lattices offset in plane have nearest
    cells up to 0.71 step apart, so the half step rule leaves up to about a fifth of a true overlap counted twice; one step,
    about 4 voxels, stays under a third of the sheet pitch, so two neighbour laminae are not merged);
  - `bin-max`: `union_area.union_of` (bins of 4 voxels, per bin the largest single sheet contribution), the house formula of
    coverage-union-1447, for comparability with the 1447 rows.
  The **unique clean area** is the SMALLEST of the three, and all three are written.
- **Largest connected piece** (a): the largest 4 neighbour connected component of one sheet's clean cells on its own lattice,
  area the sum of a_c. No join across sheets in (a); joins across sheets are (b).
- **Pitch**, measured from the sheets: for every ordered pair of the v2 map, the |d| (distance along B's normal, `pair_v2`'s
  D on foot cells, radius 24 voxels) histogram in 0.25 voxel bins is summed over all pairs; the pitch is the |d| of the
  histogram's maximum between 6 and 24 voxels (the neighbour lamina; the peak near 0 is the same lamina). Written with the
  histogram. Bar: the coincide distance (3 voxels, sheet_cross D_CO) must be under half the measured pitch, else (b) is not run.
- **Stitching** (b): two distinct sheets are joined when, in BOTH orders, the coinciding cells of `sheet_cross.pair`'s rule
  (smoothed |d| at most 3 voxels, normals within 20 degrees, connected coinciding area at least 16384 square voxels, the
  declared minimum overlap, 1.436 mm2 at 9.362 um) exist, computed from pair_v2's arrays with the same lines (known
  reference: equal to `sheet_cross.pair`'s n_coin on 20 ordered pairs, a column; a difference refuses), and the pair carries
  no counted v2 crossing or jump line in either order. Stitched sheets are the connected components of that graph. Two
  variants: any two sheets, and sheets of different seeds only (the order's wording); both written.
- **A stitched sheet's clean area**: the three dedup rules restricted to its member sheets, the smallest reported; its square:
  the largest over member sheets of square.py `largest_square` on that sheet's clean cells in mm by the smaller median step
  (a lower bound: the stitched surface is not reparametrised into one lattice here, so no square can cross a seam).

## What would make it wrong

- The a2 reference or the traced reference not reproduced: the file set or the reader is not the delivered one.
- The half-step rule above the one-step rule: impossible by construction; the self test checks the direction.
- Copies displaced by more than one step (misregistered growths of the same papyrus) are counted twice by every rule: the
  union is then an overestimate, and bin-max at 8 voxels is written beside as a sensitivity column (never the number).
- Stitching joins two laminae where the v2 map is blind (crossings shorter than L2): the stitched area would mix laminae.
  The stitched rows carry the v2 blindness in words: «no counted v2 line between the joined sheets», never «one lamina».

### (2) and (3) One lamina, operationally

The radius from the umbilicus does not measure the winding (ledger 2026-09-19, memory rule): not used. One lamina is defined
by what the sheets show about each other at the measured pitch p = 15.125 voxels (pitch.csv):
- **Pitch conflict** between a cell a (sheet A, working lattice) and a cell b (sheet B, any sheet, A itself included): the
  displacement b minus a has its component along A's normal between 0.5 p and 1.5 p in absolute value, its lateral part at
  most max(step_i, step_j) of A's working lattice, and the normals within 20 degrees (sheet_cross COS_CO); when B is A, the
  two cells must also be more than 3 p apart on A's lattice (index distance times working step), so a sheet folding back or
  wrapping around is caught and its local curvature is not. Computed with a KD tree on all working cells (clean or not:
  conservative).
- **Conflicting pair**: the cells of A in conflict with B cover at least 16384 square voxels (the same minimum as the
  coincidence), in either order. **Wrapping sheet**: a sheet whose self conflict cells cover at least 16384 square voxels.
- **(2) One winding group**: start from single sheets; wrapping sheets are left out; the join edges of stitch.csv (coinciding
  in both orders, no counted v2 line) are taken in decreasing order of the smaller coinciding area of the two orders, and two
  groups merge only when no member of one conflicts with any member of the other. So no group holds two sheets one pitch
  apart anywhere they overlap over 16384 square voxels, whether of the same seed or not. Reported: the largest group by clean
  unique area (smallest of the three dedup rules over its members), its seeds and sheets, its largest member square, a column
  with the conflicting pairs inside it (must be 0) and the number of same seed pairs inside it (allowed only because they
  coincide and do not conflict).
- **(3) One lamina piece**: on every sheet, remove its self conflict cells (mapped to the full lattice by the nearest working
  cell) from the clean cells; the largest 4 neighbour component left, over all sheets, with its area; the component is then
  checked to carry no self conflict cell (0 by construction, written).
- Both (2) and (3) are one lamina only up to what a pitch conflict over 16384 square voxels can see: two parts of a group
  more than 1.5 p apart, or overlapping less than that, are not tested; the rows say so.
- Tool `tools/lamina.py` (imports area90.py), outputs `evidence/conflicts.csv` (per ordered pair with conflict cells),
  `evidence/self-conflicts.csv`, `evidence/one-lamina.csv`. At most 6 processes, nice 10, CLOCK.lock pause, stop under 50 GB.
